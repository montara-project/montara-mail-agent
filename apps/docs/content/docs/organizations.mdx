---
title: Organizations
description: Who sees which mailboxes, and who can change what.
---

An organization holds mailboxes, its AI provider, agent bots, skills and MCP servers. Members share its mailboxes.

## Roles

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| Member | Read and manage the organization's mailboxes and mail, use the assistant. |
| Owner | Everything a member can, plus rename or delete the organization and change its bots, skills and MCP servers, and its AI provider through the API. |
| Super user | Sees and manages every organization. The account named in the server's `SUPER_USER`. |

Someone else's organization, mailbox or mail answers as not found, never as forbidden.

## Deleting

Deleting an organization moves it to the trash, where the owner can restore it. **Delete for good** removes it and everything in it.

## Activity log

Each organization keeps an audit log of what happened in it: mailboxes connected, mail sent, settings changed. You also have your own log under **Control Center › Security**. Entries are kept for a year by default.
