Every AI feature calls the model your organization configured under **Control Center › AI Providers**. Email content is treated as untrusted data in every prompt: instructions written inside a mail are never followed.

## Sorting

New inbox mail gets one of three labels:

| Label | Means |
| --- | --- |
| **Important** | A real person or business needs you: a question, a request, a deadline, a decision, money, security or a personal message. |
| **Broadcast** | One message sent to many people: newsletters, announcements, digests and marketing. |
| **Low priority** | Everything else: social notifications, receipts and automated updates. |

Agree with a label (thumbs up) to keep it as yours, or disagree to pick another. A label you chose is never changed by the AI.

## AI Email Summary

A short summary of one mail, with the key names, dates, amounts and deadlines in bold. It is kept after the first time, so opening the mail again costs nothing.

## Write with AI

Under a reply, **Use AI** opens the writing panel:

- **Quick replies:** up to three short replies suggested for the mail. Pick one to start from it.
- **Describe the reply you want** and **Write** drafts the whole reply. Type <kbd>/</kbd> for skill prompts.
- **Polish (write a draft first):** Humanize, Copy edit, and Translate reply into the mail's language in a tone you pick.

Your reply only changes when you choose **Use**.

## Skills

Skills are named instructions run on one email from the reader's **Skills** menu, or with a `/command` in the assistant. Every organization has these built in:

- Summarize email, Organize, Draft / reply email
- Humanize, Copy edit
- Reply to a prospect, Handle an objection, Reply to press
- Translate email, Translate reply

Translation reads the mail faithfully into your browser's language; tone only applies to a translated reply. Owners can turn built-in skills off and add their own: see [Bots and skills](/bots-and-skills).
