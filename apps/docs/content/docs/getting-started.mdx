## 1. Sign in

Open [app.montaramail.web.id](https://app.montaramail.web.id) and sign in with Google: your account is created on the first sign-in. If you already have an email and password account, sign in with those.

<Callout title="Running your own server?">
  The Community Edition has no sign-up: its only account is the super user set in `SUPER_USER`. See [Self-hosting](/self-hosting).
</Callout>

## 2. Create an organization

Mailboxes, models and AI settings belong to an **organization**. Create one from the organization switcher at the top of the sidebar. You become its owner, and you can belong to as many as you like, for example one for work and one for personal mail.

## 3. Choose a model

A super user adds the model your organization's AI features use under **Control Center › AI Providers**: an OpenAI- or Anthropic-compatible endpoint with its API key, or a self-hosted model. Without one, the organization uses the server's fallback model when there is one, and otherwise gets no AI features. See [AI providers](/self-hosting/llm-providers).

## 4. Connect a mailbox

Open **Accounts**, pick your provider and sign in with an app password (or OAuth, where the server offers it). The first sync takes the newest 50 messages of each folder; after that every new message arrives within the sync interval, 5 minutes by default. See [Connect mailboxes](/mailboxes).

## 5. Work through your inbox

- New inbox mail is labelled **Important**, **Low priority** or **Broadcast**; filter by label from the sidebar.
- Open a mail for an **AI Email Summary**, reply suggestions and skills such as translation.
- The **AI assistant** answers questions about your mailbox and proposes replies for you to approve.
